Meet the Dentist

Meet Dr. T.J. Fowler

A Knoxville native providing restorative, cosmetic, preventive, and family dental care with a community-focused approach.

Knoxville Roots. Professional Dental Care.

Dr. Fowler was born and raised in Knoxville, Tennessee, and his family has been part of the community for over a century.

He attended Knoxville Catholic High School before earning his Bachelor of Science degree in Microbiology from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville.

Dr. Fowler later attended the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry in Memphis, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Following dental school, he completed a General Practice Residency through the University of Tennessee Graduate School of Medicine at UT Hospital.

After working as an associate dentist in Oak Ridge, Dr. Fowler opened his Knoxville dental practice in late 2007 and continues serving the dental needs of the community today.

Outside the office, Dr. Fowler enjoys golf, music, and spending time with family and friends.
