Meet the Dentist

Meet Dr. T.J. Fowler

A Knoxville native providing restorative, cosmetic, family, and preventive dental care with a personal, community-focused approach.

Dr. Fowler Photo

Knoxville Roots. Professional Dental Care.

Hello! My name is T.J. Fowler, DDS. I was born and raised here in Knoxville, Tennessee, and my family has been a part of this community for over a century.

I attended Knoxville Catholic High School, graduating in 1997. I later attended the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, where I received a Bachelor of Science degree in Microbiology in May 2001.

After completing my undergraduate studies, I moved to Memphis, Tennessee, to attend the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry, where I received my Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in May 2005.

After dental school, I was accepted into a postgraduate training program known as a General Practice Residency by the University of Tennessee Graduate School of Medicine at UT Hospital. I completed my residency in August 2006 and then worked as an associate dentist in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, before starting my own practice in Knoxville.

We opened our doors in late December of 2007. On a lighter note, I am an avid golfer, I try to play the guitar, and I love being a part of this community.

I am a product of the University of Tennessee from start to finish, and I look forward to many more years serving the dental needs of this great city.
