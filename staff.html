Dental Services

Comprehensive Dental Care in Knoxville

T.J. Fowler DDS provides restorative, cosmetic, family, and preventive dental care in a calm and professional environment designed around patient comfort and long-term oral health.

Services Offered

General, Restorative & Cosmetic Dentistry

The office provides a wide range of dental services for individuals and families throughout Knoxville and surrounding communities.

General & Family Dentistry

Preventive dental care including exams, cleanings, x-rays, and long-term oral health support for patients of all ages.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dental services focused on improving smile appearance, confidence, and overall aesthetics.

Whitening & Veneers

Professional whitening and veneer treatment options designed to improve tooth color, shape, and appearance.

Implant Dentistry

Dental implant solutions intended to restore both appearance and function after tooth loss.

Crowns & Bridges

Restorative treatment options for damaged, weakened, or missing teeth using durable dental restorations.

Root Canal Therapy

Treatment focused on relieving pain and helping preserve natural teeth whenever possible.

Routine Cleanings

Preventive cleanings and maintenance appointments designed to support long-term oral health.

Gum Disease Treatment

Periodontal treatment and dental hygiene support for gum health and disease prevention.

Nitrous Oxide Sedation

Comfort-focused options available for patients who experience anxiety or nervousness during dental visits.

Pediatric Dentistry

Friendly dental care for children and families in a calm and welcoming environment.
